## 2. History

Kitui School's history is a story of colonial-era origins, local African effort and steady
elevation to national status. The project website (About page) presents it in four eras:

### 2.1 Colonial Foundations (1908–1939)
- **1908** — Established by the British colonial government as an intermediate school near the
  Kitui Police Station. It is described as the **first secular Government African School in the
  colony**, built with strong backing from local Akamba communities, who volunteered land, food
  and labour. (Some accounts date it 1909 as a primary school.)
- **1938–39** — The school outgrew its original site and was relocated to its present permanent
  grounds. It was officially renamed **Campbell Academy** in honour of Provincial Commissioner
  Mr. Campbell, who supported its development.

### 2.2 Expansion & Co-Education (1940s–1950s)
- **1942** — Firmly established; temporarily operated as a co-educational facility, with girls
  also in attendance.
- **1947–49** — Officially transitioned into a **regional boarding school** as the first permanent
  student dormitories were completed (Wikipedia and the official school site cite **1948**).
- **1957** — Upgraded to a **full secondary school**; its inaugural O-Level class graduated in **1960**.

### 2.3 Post-Independence & Academic Elevation (1960s–1980s)
- **1971** — Introduced the advanced A-Level tier (Forms 5 and 6) to meet the demands of a newly
  independent Kenya.
- **1980** — Phased out A-Level classes and re-aligned into the standard **8-4-4** secondary system
  following nationwide reforms.

### 2.4 National School Status (Present)
- Today the school hosts ~1,500 students drawn from all 47 counties and is recognised nationally
  for an ultra-competitive academic environment with a strong focus on STEM alongside sports, arts
  and service.
- Elevated to **National School** status by the Ministry of Education on the strength of its KCSE
  performances. It is now a **C1 senior school** under the CBC offering Grade 10–12 pathways.

### 2.5 Recent Milestones
- **12 July 2025** — Launched its ambitious **2023–2028 Strategic Plan** in a grand ceremony themed
  *"We Serve a Mighty God"*, held alongside Prize Giving Day and the Annual General Meeting. Chief
  guest: Kenya's Secondary Education Director, **Dr. William K. Sugut**. Also present: Kitui County
  Education Director **Dr. Hassan Khalif**, Parents Association Chairperson **Jude Mawia** and BOM
  Chairman **John Kyalo**.
- **2025** — Hosted the opening of the 2025 KCSE examinations, honoured by Education CS
  **Julius Migos Ogamba**.
- **July 2026** — Hosted the Eastern Region Term Two Championships (schools games).

## 6. CBC Senior School & Pathways (Grades 10–12)

As a **C1 National senior school** under the Competency-Based Curriculum, Kitui School offers all
three CBC pathways:

1. **STEM** — Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics (divided into Pure Sciences, Applied
   Sciences and Technical Studies).
2. **Social Sciences** — Humanities, Business & Social Sciences (Languages & Literature, and
   Humanities & Business Studies).
3. **Arts & Sports Science** — Creative Arts and Physical Education (Arts, and Sports).

- **Grade 10 placement** is done centrally by the Ministry of Education via the Grade 10 Selection
  System; the school declares capacity and subject combinations (STEM compulsory; min. ~15 learners
  per subject combination).

**Sports:**
- **Rugby**: Kitui County rugby-15s champions (2024; beat Gai Secondary in the final; previously
  defeated Kiviu B 79–0 in county action). Eastern Region finalists 2026 (lost 29–3 to All Saints
  Embu). Hosted the Eastern Region Term Two Championships in July 2026.

## 11. Discrepancies to Reconcile in the Project

The current website contains a few internal inconsistencies that a future editor should harmonise:

1. **Founding year**: Homepage/Navbar/Footer say **"Since 1938"** (and homepage stats say
   "Year Founded: 1938"), while the About page correctly narrates founding in **1908** and relocation
   in 1938. Official school material and Wikipedia use **1908**.
2. **Enrolment**: Homepage says **2,300+ students**; About page says **1,500+**; Wikipedia ~1,500;
   other directories ~840.
3. **KCSE 2025 mean**: Homepage stats say **9.80**; About table says **9.72**; official site and
   third-party portals say **9.718** (some round to 9.7/9.71).
4. **KCSE 2025 national rank**: official site says **13th**; several education portals list Kitui
   at **20th** (9.71).
5. **Teaching staff**: Homepage says **120+**; not corroborated by external sources.
6. **Contact hours**: Management page says Mon–Fri 8:00–16:30; Contact page says Mon–Fri 8:00–17:00.
7. **BOM chairperson**: site lists Mr. David Musyoka; 2025 press names John Kyalo.
8. The homepage KCSE 2025 highlights card says "Mean grade 9.80 with 99.6% university transition",
   "40 scored A, 80 scored B+", "260 candidates" — none of which match the About/Academics tables
   (17 A, 109 B+, 354 candidates, 98.59%).
